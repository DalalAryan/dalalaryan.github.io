Engineered 30 multi-correlation features from 10K segmented lesion images with OpenCV and scikit-learn, then stacked XGBoost, LightGBM, SVM, and CART base learners under an XGBoost meta-classifier. Reached 88.4% accuracy and 0.81 macro F1. Code: [DalalAryan/CancerPredictionModel](https://github.com/DalalAryan/CancerPredictionModel).
