Research with Prof. Guido Montufar on LLM formal theorem proving (Lean, LeanDojo), autoformalization, and RLVR training, through the NeurIPS 2025 MATH-AI Workshop. Expanded a 78-example dataset to 458 instruction pairs and fine-tuned Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct with 4-bit QLoRA for algebraic-geometry code generation.
