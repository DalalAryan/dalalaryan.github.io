Hi! I'm Aryan, an undergraduate at UCLA studying mathematics and computer engineering. In this post, I discuss the [Physical-Extended Church-Turing Thesis (Alonzo Church, Alan Turing, Stephen Kleene, 1930s)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Church–Turing_thesis). 

This post was mostly motivated by curiosity. In class, a short discussion over the philosophical realizations of the Church-Turing Thesis (CTT) and its validity (no proof!) in both the classical and quantum sense seemed intriguing to me. It's believed that the Physical-Extended CTT is true in a classical sense and false in a quantum sense (but why? that's what we discuss in the blog :D), however, one can easily construct (or I suppose, extend) a Quantum-Extended Church-Turing Thesis which allows CTT to be true in a quantum sense as well (we discuss this too).
